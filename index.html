Nordic venture · 2020 — 2024

VC
Challenge

The training ground for the first funds of the Nordics.

~100 emerging managers. Five countries. One ecosystem.

Almost half of the Nordic fund managers were investing their first fund. Nobody was teaching them how. So we built the place where the region’s next generation of VCs learned to raise, build and run a fund.

01 — The arc

  1. 2020 Oslo

    Born at Startup Norway as a programme for Norway’s aspiring fund managers.

  2. 2021 Helsinki

    First demo day: Norwegian emerging funds pitch to investors at the Arctic15 LP Summit.

  3. 2022 The Nordics

    Goes Nordic with Nordic Innovation, with partners in all five countries.

  4. 2023 Copenhagen

    The first Nordic cohort: 25 emerging fund teams from Iceland to Finland.

  5. 2024 Closed

    The last cohort graduates. The challenge ends. The funds keep investing.
