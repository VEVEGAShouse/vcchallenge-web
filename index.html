Nordic venture · 2020 — 2024

VC
Challenge

The training ground for the first funds of the Nordics.

~100 emerging managers. Four programmes. One ecosystem.

Almost half of the Nordic fund managers were investing their first fund. Nobody was teaching them how. So we built the place where the region’s next generation of VCs learned to raise, build and run a fund.

01 — The arc

  1. 2020 Oslo · Prototype

    It starts as a bridge between Norwegian startups and venture capital.

  2. 2021 Norway

    The first VC Challenge: Norway’s aspiring fund managers learn to build a fund, and pitch to LPs in Helsinki.

  3. 2022 Nordic I

    The first Nordic edition: 19 emerging funds from all five countries.

  4. 2023 Nordic II · Copenhagen

    26 venture teams, 44 managers. 18 of the teams go on to build a fund.

  5. 23/24 Nordic III

    15 teams, 30 managers, from the founders of Iceland’s first unicorn to Germany’s deep-tech investors.

  6. 2024 Closed

    The last cohort graduates. The challenge ends. The funds keep investing.
