  1. 00 · The gap

    Half of the Nordic fund managers were on their first fund.

    And there was no school for it. Virginia E. Vegas decided to build one.

  2. 01 · The door

    We opened it, across five countries.

    Founders, business angels, corporates, family offices, public investors. Anyone with a thesis and the nerve to raise.

  3. 02 · The room

    Three Nordic cohorts. Around 100 managers.

    Expert-led sessions on sourcing and dealflow, portfolio construction, fundraising strategy and fund operations.

  4. 03 · The circle

    The people who run the industry, in the room.

    VCs, lawyers, family offices, fund administrators, and the state LPs who decide where the capital goes.

  5. 04 · The leap

    70% decided to launch.

    In 2023 alone: 18 funds in the making, over €200m in target capital, more than 350 companies to back.

  6. 05 · The network

    One Nordic network. Still investing.

    The challenge ended in 2024. The funds it started did not.
